## What the user base actually wants (evidence)

- Muscle loss is the #1 fear that no tracker addresses properly: up to 40% of weight lost on semaglutide/tirzepatide can be lean mass. Protein tracking to a daily target is the single most-cited intervention.
- Side-effect load is the #1 churn driver from the drug itself: nausea (37%), fatigue (17%), vomiting (16%), constipation (15%), diarrhoea (13%), plus under-reported cycle changes and temperature swings (410k Reddit posts analysed).
- People stop logging food by week two. Meal scan solves friction but MAX gates it at 10 scans/day.
- Users want: never miss a dose, site rotation, side-effect diary, hydration, non-scale victories, mood/energy, doctor-ready export.

## Gaps nobody owns (our openings)

1. **Education as you go.** Every app is a form with charts. None teaches. First reconstitution, first nausea, first plateau, first missed dose: each is a teachable moment nobody uses.
2. **Sleep and recovery as a pillar.** Sleep drives hunger hormones, cravings and lean-mass retention. Everyone shows a "8.5 hrs" tile; nobody correlates it with shot day, protein or weight trend, and nobody has a calm night-time UI.
3. **Lean-mass protection loop.** Protein target + resistance-training nudges + body-composition trend, framed as one goal ("lose fat, keep muscle").
4. **UK-first, then global.** Metric, Mounjaro/Wegovy naming, NHS and private-pharmacy pathways, MHRA-safe language. Every incumbent is US-centric.
5. **Honest pricing.** No weekly subscriptions. Annual + lifetime. Free tier that is genuinely usable.
6. **Privacy-first.** Local-first data, no forced account, one-tap export/delete. Injection and weight data is sensitive; incumbents bury it in the cloud.
